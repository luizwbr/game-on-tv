    How to Connect

    1. Install the Game-on-TV app on your Samsung Tizen TV
    2. Make sure your TV and PC are on the same local network
    3. Open the app on your TV and select "Discover PC"
    4. Your PC should appear in the list - select it to connect
    5. Click "Start Streaming" above to begin streaming
    💡 Tip: For best performance, use a wired Ethernet connection for both your PC and TV.
